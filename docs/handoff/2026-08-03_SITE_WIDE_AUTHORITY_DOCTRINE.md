# Site-wide authority doctrine handoff

Date: 2026-08-03
Branch: `codex/site-wide-authority-doctrine`

## Outcome

The public site now presents Hyperion as a continuity infrastructure company rather than a founder-led portfolio. The shared public doctrine is centralized, public routes use an assessment-first entry posture, and district pages expose source, context, and authority boundaries without implying execution access.

## Public contract

- Capture state.
- Preserve provenance.
- Expose context.
- Gate authority.
- Route action.
- Learn from reality contact.
- Facts are source-bound. Meaning is context-bound. Actions are authority-bound.

## Changed surfaces

- Company-first City Gate and global footer.
- Continuity method, architecture map, systems directory, contact, founder context, and intake language.
- Explicit public authority contracts for every City district.
- Maturity labels for live, development, architecture, and review lanes.
- SEO metadata, public retrieval corpus, operator corpus, and compiled route artifacts.
- Doctrine regression tests and the top-level repository check.

## Preserved boundaries

- Public surfaces remain compiled-only and source-opaque.
- Public intake creates review work; it does not create a quote, contract, deployment, purchase, or access grant.
- Forge and Card Studio remain held-for-review, operator-gated workflows.
- Founder pages remain available as operator context without standing in for the company narrative.
